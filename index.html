Shuyao Shang

I'm a second-year Ph.D. student at the NLPR, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA) in Beijing, supervised by Prof. Tieniu Tan and Prof. Zhaoxiang Zhang.

My research interests span General World Models and Video Generation Models. I am committed to developing intelligent agents that can autonomously evolve intelligence in open-world exploration, and "Pursuing the Nature of Intelligence" .
